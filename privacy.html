KnowFlow Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

What KnowFlow is

KnowFlow is a personal knowledge assistant. It answers questions using files you upload and, if you connect it, your Gmail messages.

Data we access

  • Gmail (read-only): if you connect Gmail, KnowFlow reads the sender, date, subject and body of your messages using the gmail.readonly permission. It cannot send, modify or delete email. Messages Gmail labels as Promotions, Social, Forums or Spam are skipped.
  • Files you upload: the files themselves and the text extracted from them.
  • Account details: your name, email address and a hashed password for signing in to KnowFlow.

How we use it

Only to build a private searchable index for your account and to answer your own questions, showing the passages used. We do not sell your data, use it for advertising, or build profiles from it.

Where it is stored and who processes it

  • Indexed text, uploaded files and settings are stored in the database and file storage of the KnowFlow installation you use. Gmail access tokens are encrypted before storage.
  • To create the searchable index and to write answers, the text of your content and your questions is sent to Google's Gemini API for processing. That processing is governed by Google's terms for the Gemini API.
  • We do not share your data with any other third party, except where required by law.
  • No person reads your messages, except with your permission (for example, when you ask for support), where needed for security or abuse prevention, or where the law requires it.

Google API Services User Data Policy

KnowFlow's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Your choices and deletion

  • Disconnect Gmail at any time from the app's Connectors page, or revoke access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • To have your account, indexed content and uploaded files deleted, email the address below and we will delete them.
