KnowFlow

A private assistant that answers questions from your own documents and email, with the source shown for every answer.

What it does

KnowFlow lets you upload files (PDF, Markdown, plain text) and, if you choose, connect your Gmail account in read-only mode. It builds a private, searchable index of that content for your account and answers your questions from it. Each answer shows the exact passage it came from, so you can check it instead of trusting it.

How Gmail access works

  • KnowFlow asks for read-only Gmail access. It cannot send, delete, or change email.
  • Emails Gmail labels as Promotions, Social, Forums or Spam are skipped.
  • You can disconnect Gmail at any time from the app, or revoke access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Read the Privacy Policy for exactly what is stored and who processes it.
